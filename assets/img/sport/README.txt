VITAS — sport card photography
==============================

Three files go in this folder. Until they exist, the site falls back to the
illustrated SVGs in ../sport-*.svg, so nothing breaks if you deploy first.

Required files
--------------
  hyrox.webp      1600 x 1067   (3:2 landscape)
  hyrox-800.webp   800 x  534
  padel.webp      1600 x 1067
  padel-800.webp   800 x  534
  running.webp    1600 x 1067
  running-800.webp 800 x  534

Export at WebP quality 82-86. Each file should land under ~180 KB.

Where to get them (free for commercial use, no attribution required)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  unsplash.com  /  pexels.com  — both licence for commercial use.
  Read each photo's licence line before you download; a small number of
  Unsplash images are marked editorial-only.

Search terms that return usable frames:
  hyrox    -> "sled push gym", "wall ball workout", "functional fitness
              competition", "sandbag lunge". Do NOT use photos of actual
              HYROX-branded events — see the trademark note below.
  padel    -> "padel court", "padel racket player", "paddle tennis glass court"
  running  -> "running track intervals", "trail running legs", "runner city
              morning". Prefer a frame with legs/calves in it, since that is
              what the page is about.

What a good frame looks like here
---------------------------------
  - Real effort, mid-movement. Not a posed studio shot.
  - Warm or neutral light. The site palette is chalk + orange; cold blue
    gym photography fights it.
  - Space on one side. The card crops to 3:2 and overlays nothing, but the
    grid is narrow on mobile, so a tight subject reads better than a wide one.
  - Hong Kong or East Asian subjects where possible. The site is a HK brand
    and the current illustrations are neutral; photography that is visibly
    all-Western is a step backwards on relevance.
  - No visible competitor product, no medical/taping imagery, no injury or
    pain expressions. Injury imagery pulls the brand back toward the
    medicine-cabinet positioning the site was rewritten to escape.

Trademark note — HYROX
----------------------
HYROX is a registered trademark of Upsolut Sports AG. Using the word as a
descriptive reference to the sport format is normal, but do not use event
photography, the HYROX logo, race branding, or anything that implies an
official partnership or endorsement. Generic functional-fitness gym imagery
is the safe choice unless you have written permission.

Model releases
--------------
Unsplash and Pexels do not guarantee model releases. For a commercial
advertisement in Hong Kong featuring recognisable faces, either pick frames
where faces are not identifiable, or commission a shoot with a signed release.
This is the main reason to shoot your own eventually.
