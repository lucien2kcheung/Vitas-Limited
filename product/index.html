Inside the tube

The three that matter

Eucalyptus Eucalyptus globulus Gives the cream its quiet coolness on the skin — noticeable, never fierce.
Grape seed Vitis vinifera A light, fast-absorbing oil pressed from wine-grape seeds. It is why the cream slides and then disappears.
Niaouli Melaleuca viridiflora A soft green aromatic from the same family as tea tree. It rounds the eucalyptus so the cream smells like a plant, not a pharmacy.
