Why we cut the claims

Why VITAS

Four reasons, none of them magic

The shelf is crowded and cheap. Here is the honest case for paying a little more for this one.

It does not follow you around

No methyl salicylate, no camphor — the two things that give a traditional medicated rub its carrying smell. You can use VITAS at 3pm in an open-plan office, between meetings, or on the MTR, and the person next to you will not know.

It absorbs before you dress

A light grape-seed base sinks in within a minute and leaves no shine, so you can put a shirt back on and get on with your day. Most recovery rubs leave you greasy; this one does not.

Clean by formulation, not by accident

Three plant actives — grape seed, niaouli and eucalyptus — with no hormones, no steroids and no artificial fragrance. Made in France — produced with EEC GMP standard, the same specification for over twenty years.

Honest about what it is

A cosmetic massage gel that feels warming before effort and cooling after it. Not a medicine, not a miracle. We would rather earn you with the truth than sell you a story — see Our Approach.

Inside the tube

The three that matter

Eucalyptus Eucalyptus globulus Gives the cream its quiet coolness on the skin — noticeable, never fierce.
Grape seed Vitis vinifera A light, fast-absorbing oil pressed from wine-grape seeds. It is why the cream slides and then disappears.
Niaouli Melaleuca viridiflora A soft green aromatic from the same family as tea tree. It rounds the eucalyptus so the cream smells like a plant, not a pharmacy.
