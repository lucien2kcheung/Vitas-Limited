Three plants

A short ingredient list, on purpose

Grape seed carries it, niaouli warms it, eucalyptus cools it. Each one has its own page.

The cool one Eucalyptus Eucalyptus globulus Gives the cream its quiet coolness on the skin — noticeable, never fierce.
The carrier Grape seed Vitis vinifera A light, fast-absorbing oil pressed from wine-grape seeds. It is why the cream slides and then disappears.
The rounding note Niaouli Melaleuca viridiflora A soft green aromatic from the same family as tea tree. It rounds the eucalyptus so the cream smells like a plant, not a pharmacy.
