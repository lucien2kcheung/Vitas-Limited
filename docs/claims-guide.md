# VITAS — Compliance & Claims Guide

Source: *VITAS Updated Website Content & Creative Direction* (2026), §10.
This is the authoritative claims discipline for all website, social and campaign
copy. It supersedes the older "Activate Circulation. Accelerate Recovery."
positioning. When writing anything for VITAS, check it against the table below
before publishing.

## Mandatory website disclaimer

> This product is a cosmetic for massage and external use. References to
> "warm-up", "wind-down", "prime", "perform", "recover", and warming/cooling
> describe the intended training ritual and sensory experience during massage.
> They do not represent medical, therapeutic or performance-enhancement claims.
> This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.

繁中：本產品為供按摩及外用的化妝品。「熱身」、「收操」、「Prime」、「Perform」、「Recover」以及溫熱／清涼等字眼，只用作描述運動流程及按摩時的感官體驗，並不代表任何醫療、治療或提升運動表現的聲稱。本產品並非用作診斷、治療、治癒或預防任何疾病。

Lives in the footer of every page (`BRAND.disclaimer` in `src/data.mjs`).

## Safety / caution

> For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. Discontinue use if irritation
> occurs. Not suitable for children under 6, pregnant or breastfeeding women.

Note: the source document says "children under 3". This site uses "under 6" to
stay consistent with the fuller cautions on `/how-to-use/` and to be more
conservative, which is permitted. Pick one number sitewide if you change it.

## Claims language guide

| Use | Avoid | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Warming / cooling sensation | Stimulates circulation / removes lactic acid | Medical mechanism claim |
| Pre + post workout ritual | Accelerates recovery / enhances performance | Outcome / performance claim |
| Massage into tired areas | Treats muscle pain / inflammation | Therapeutic claim |
| Plant-based actives | 100% natural | Absolute composition claim unless fully substantiated |
| Formulated without [specific ingredient] | Zero toxins / toxin-free | Vague / absolute claim |
| Made in France · Produced with EEC GMP standard | EU GMP certified / clinically proven | Use only the exact verified manufacturing wording |

## Approved brand lines

- PRE + POST WORKOUT / 運動前後
- WARM UP. WIND DOWN. / 熱身備戰．放鬆收操。
- PRIME. PERFORM. RECOVER. / 喚醒．投入．收操。
- GRAPE SEED · NIAOULI · EUCALYPTUS / 葡萄籽 · 綠花白千層 · 尤加利
- MADE IN FRANCE / 法國製造

**Do not use** "Activate Circulation. Accelerate Recovery." in the new brand
system. Keep all copy focused on the training ritual, sensory experience,
texture, formulation and verified manufacturing facts.

## KOL / influencer guidance

**DO:** "I use VITAS as part of my pre- and post-workout ritual. I like the
warming sensation before training and the cooling sensation when I wind down
afterwards."

**DON'T:** "VITAS speeds up recovery, boosts circulation, prevents injury,
removes lactic acid or cures muscle pain."

Sponsored content must be clearly identified as advertising / collaboration
where required.
