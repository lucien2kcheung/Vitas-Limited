The cool one

Eucalyptus

Gives the cream its quiet coolness on the skin — noticeable, never fierce.

Eucalyptus globulus

Eucalyptus globulus leaf oil is rich in 1,8-cineole, the compound behind its clean, faintly camphoraceous scent and the cool feeling it leaves behind. In VITAS it is dosed for comfort rather than shock: enough to register as you rub it in, not enough to announce itself to the person sitting next to you.

What you feel

A cool note that arrives a few seconds after you rub it in and fades over the next few minutes. It is a sensation on the skin, not a change in the temperature of the muscle underneath.

Why not menthol or camphor

Both are stronger, cheaper and carry a medicated smell across a room. Eucalyptus gives a gentler version of the same cool sensation, which is the whole point of a cream you can use at your desk.

Where it comes from

Steam-distilled from the leaves of the blue gum tree. In the finished cream it appears on the INCI list as Eucalyptus Globulus Leaf Oil.

Formulation

What is deliberately not in it

Most muscle rubs work by shouting. Ours is built around the things it leaves out — which is why you can use it at 3pm in an open-plan office.

  • No methyl salicylate
  • No camphor
  • No hormones
  • No steroids
  • No medicinal smell

The other two

What else is in the tube

The carrier Grape seed Vitis vinifera A light, fast-absorbing oil pressed from wine-grape seeds. It is why the cream slides and then disappears.
The rounding note Niaouli Melaleuca viridiflora A soft green aromatic from the same family as tea tree. It rounds the eucalyptus so the cream smells like a plant, not a pharmacy.
