For running clubs

For the legs that feel like cement after intervals.

Three to five runs a week, one of them hard. Whether you run Happy Valley loops or the harbourfront, the routine that keeps you consistent is the one that takes two minutes and does not smell.

Warm up.Wind down.

VITAS provides a warming sensation when massaged into muscles before training, and a cooling sensation afterwards. These sensory experiences are part of your active warm-up and wind-down routine.

Cool down

After the cool-down jog: long upward strokes on the calves and quads while your heart rate settles. It travels well in a running belt or a club bag.
