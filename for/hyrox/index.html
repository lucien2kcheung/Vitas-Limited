For Hyrox athletes

From burpees to broad jumps — keep your legs feeling light.

Eight workouts, eight kilometres, and a sled that does not care how you feel. The work between stations is where the session is won — and the two minutes before and ten minutes after are the part you control.

Activate Circulation.Accelerate Recovery.

VITAS provides a warming sensation when massaged into muscles before training, and a cooling sensation afterwards. These sensory experiences are part of your active warm-up and wind-down routine.

Shop performance How to use it
Before · Activate

Warm up

Ten minutes before the first station, massage into quads, calves and shoulders. The warming sensation is your cue that the muscle has had attention — then go and do the dynamic warm-up properly.

After · Accelerate

Cool down

After the last sled push, shower, then work upward along the legs in long slow strokes. Cooling, non-greasy, absorbed before you put your kit back on.
